RASA Student Lifecycle Management

Operations guide

Finding records

Use the top search for a student, or open Students to filter by year, status, course and trainer. Select rows before exporting only those records.

Workbook imports

Client workbooks are staged, validated and reconciled before production import. Source cells remain available for audit and low-confidence values stay in the review queue.

Student lifecycle

Open a student to review course, fees, imported attendance, project, certificate, HR and source history. Changes require an authenticated database connection.

Exports

Students and Reports provide working CSV downloads. Exports respect current filters and escape spreadsheet formulas.
